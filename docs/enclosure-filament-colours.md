# Enclosure filament colour shortlist

Research date: 2026-09-25. These are purchase candidates, not recorded purchases. Prices below are the displayed web listings, include the listing's VAT and exclude shipping; cached listings and destination tax can differ from checkout. Stock has not been guaranteed.

## Project palette

The current palette in `src/BridgePortalUi.h` and `assets/battery-sentinel-orion-logo.svg` is navy `#071521`, mint/teal `#68E3D1`, light blue `#8ABFFD`, and near-white `#F1F7F8`.

Recommended two-colour arrangement: navy PETG body and lid, mint PETG flush logo and lettering. White is an alternative marking colour. The current lid groups logo and lettering into one contrasting material; independently colouring them would require another artwork split.
