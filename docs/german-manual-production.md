## Findings and physical acceptance still open

- Follow the R5 power-review/module-selection data. SERVICE must physically disconnect the external module-positive supply before USB; disconnect USB before selecting RUN. Do not invent a left/right switch direction or an enclosure IP rating.
- Phone-file OTA is included on page 18. Older installed firmware may lack that section or the password controls added in 1.1.3. Real device upload/abort/restart acceptance is not asserted by this manual.
- Password change/recovery and blue LED timing require physical acceptance on the target hardware. The indicator follows AP-start state, a usable local IP and the HTTPS task, with no artificial readiness delay. A phone's Wi-Fi scan refresh and ROM/bootloader behavior before application setup are outside this indication.
- The R5 hardware plan reserves 12 LEN; the `orion_dongle` firmware currently uses `0xff` in `include/config.h`. The manual describes 12 as a planning budget, not a verified bus declaration. Reconcile this before a hardware release.
- No finished-board electrical/thermal, isolation, enclosure fit, RF or Garmin acceptance was performed during document creation. The handover form records these remaining checks without implying certification.
- Actual voltage warning timing, missing-data recovery, network UTC acquisition/conflicts and Garmin activation, repeats, local acknowledgment, clearing, interruption and restart require physical acceptance. The built-in Garmin Unit Voltage alarm does not establish separate warnings for arbitrary received battery instances.
- Recheck content, screenshots, source hashes and the completed hardware when releasing a new manual edition. Firmware and CAD changes remain outside this documentation task.
