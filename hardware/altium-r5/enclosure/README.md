## Progress and open checks

CAD and native slicing checks are complete. Confirm the purchased inserts and screws, measure the delivered cable, print the trial pieces, and check the actual board and USB plug fit. Test cable retention and the empty enclosure for the intended splash exposure before fitting electronics. Closed-case temperature, radio reception and repeated service cycles remain physical acceptance work. No IP rating is claimed.
